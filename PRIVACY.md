# Privacy

Salah Bar for Windows doesn't collect, store or send any personal data. It has no
accounts, ads, tracking or analytics.

- **Location.** When you allow it, Salah Bar asks Windows for your approximate location to
  calculate prayer times and the Qibla where you are. The location stays on your PC: it is
  matched to a city name using a list that ships with the app, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings** are saved on your PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SalahBar`.
- **Internet.** Salah Bar connects to GitHub only to check for and download updates (the
  direct-download version), and to download adhan recordings you choose from the online
  library. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.
- **Microphone and camera.** "Stay quiet during calls" checks whether another app is using the
  microphone or camera (the same information Windows shows in its privacy indicator). Salah
  Bar never uses the microphone or camera itself.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
